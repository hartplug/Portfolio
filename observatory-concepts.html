NEW DIRECTION / THREE CONCEPT ROUTES

CONCEPT A — SYSTEM CORE

Composition: One clear central control plane; six domains radiate from it in ordered tiers.
Strength: Immediate hierarchy and legible relationships.
Risk: Could still read as a hub diagram if nodes become too evenly radial.

CONCEPT B — INFRASTRUCTURE SPACE

Composition: A navigable spatial field—staggered compute, data and security structures linked by traffic paths.
Strength: Most immersive sense of depth, independent pieces, and moving information.
Risk: Needs careful mobile framing so it doesn’t become small architecture clutter.

CONCEPT C — ARCHITECTURAL GRID

Composition: Deliberate, load-bearing planes at different depths; routes bridge layers instead of orbiting a central point.
Strength: Strongest engineered quality and clearest visual grammar, least planet-like.
Risk: If too rectilinear it can feel diagrammatic rather than spatial.

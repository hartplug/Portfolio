# Visual Contract — Premium Floating AWS Orbit Hero

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Near-black cinematic infrastructure environment. Quiet luxury, editorial restraint, technical credibility. The current AWS / cloud / workflows / IT concept stays recognizable. The scene floats naturally against a continuous page background; no rectangular frame, panel, card, or container edge around the 3D scene.

## 2. Color
- Environment: charcoal/near-black, continuous with surrounding page.
- Primary scene highlight: muted champagne gold.
- Secondary light: sparse, very dim cool blue and warm copper points.
- Preserve contrast and legibility; no neon or saturated cyberpunk colors.

## 3. Typography
Use the portfolio's existing restrained sans and mono fonts. Labels are small, legible, spaced uppercase, not oversized. Keep the identity, role, tagline and existing content unchanged. Keep the positioning promise “SECURE FOUNDATIONS · RELIABLE DEPLOYMENTS · CLEAR HANDOVER” above the hero content/status area as currently placed.

## 4. Spacing & Grid
Keep broad negative space; scale the orbit up. Maintain existing responsive page gutters and header/statusbar. Avoid cards, pills, framed boxes and crowded labels.

## 5. Layout & Composition
Promote the existing orbital globe to the visual anchor. Position the sphere and orbital paths to the right of existing identity/copy on desktop, with a balanced stacked composition on phone. Labels CLOUD, WORKFLOWS and IT SOLUTIONS appear as floating text near subtle nodes/paths, not filled controls. Keep all existing hero text, links and buttons; do not change unrelated sections.

## 6. Components
Reuse the existing `.home-orbit`, Three.js canvas, existing service controls, and fallback. Remove the orbit's rectangular boundary. Retain accessible label/control semantics and functional touch/drag interaction. Use subtle nodes, layered curves, depth and particles as part of the scene, not UI decoration.

## 7. Motion & Interaction
Keep restrained autonomous orbit, drag/swipe rotation, and label selection. No continuous high-energy flashes or fast motion. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; support WebGL fallback and dispose scene resources.

## 8. Voice & Brand
Preserve Philip Taiwo / AWS cloud & IT solutions identity, approved hero copy, and existing claims exactly. Maintain premium enterprise-consulting tone without adding new claims.

## 9. Anti-patterns
No enclosing rectangle, card, rounded panel, thick border, pill UI, dashboard appearance, neon/cyberpunk colors, oversized decorative headline, gratuitous extra elements, or edits to sections beyond the hero/orbit and related styles. Preserve a restorable snapshot of the current live version before experimentation.
